# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TrustBridge to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or nationality. Contributors help a humanitarian project; we expect the community to reflect that.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and constructive
- Give and accept feedback graciously
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming otherwise
- Credit other people's work
- Keep discussions focused on the project

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or off-topic noise
- Submitting fake, duplicated, automated or plagiarised contributions, or misrepresenting work as your own
- Publishing other people's private information
- Attempting to manipulate assignments, reviews or any reward system

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Repeated or serious violations lead to a permanent ban.

To report a concern privately, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, marking the report as a conduct issue. Reports are handled confidentially.
